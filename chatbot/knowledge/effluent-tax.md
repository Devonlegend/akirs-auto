# Effluent Tax and Pollution Discharge Fee — Akwa Ibom State

## Overview

Effluent tax is a fee charged to companies for discharging waste into sewers,
reflecting the financial and economic costs of pollution. Effluent and pollution
discharge charges are designed to reduce water pollution and encourage companies
to adopt pollution control strategies. The Akwa Ibom State Internal Revenue
Service (AKIRS) collects effluent discharge tax within the state.

## How it works

Effluent charges are based on the type and level of materials discharged. They
can be charged on a uniform basis within a region or at a national level. The
aim is to internalise the social cost of pollution, making it more efficient and
equitable to reduce pollution compared with traditional regulatory approaches
alone. AKIRS's Tax Legislations page lists an Akwa Ibom State Environmental and
Waste Management Law and a "Procedures for Payment of Pollution Discharge Fee".

## Who it applies to

Businesses and companies operating in Akwa Ibom State that discharge effluent or
waste into sewers, water bodies or the environment. Operators in waste
management and related sectors should also check registration and payment
requirements.

## Rates and basis

The charge is based on the type and level of materials discharged. Akwa Ibom
State's specific effluent/pollution discharge rates are set under the state's
environmental and waste management law and its gazetted procedures; confirm the
current rate and scope with AKIRS.

## How to pay

AKIRS publishes a "Procedures for Payment of Pollution Discharge Fee". Since
February 2026, all state taxes and levies must be paid through **Akwa Remit**
(akwaremit.akwaibomstate.gov.ng), the State's approved electronic payment
platform. Taxpayers can also register and file through the IbomTax portal
(ibomtax.net) or visit an AKIRS tax office.
