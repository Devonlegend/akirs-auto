# Taxpayer Identification — AISTIN / Tax ID — Akwa Ibom State

## Overview

The Akwa Ibom State Taxpayer Identification Number (AISTIN), also referred to as
the Taxpayer ID or Tax ID, is the unique identifier that the Akwa Ibom State
Internal Revenue Service (AKIRS) uses to register and track a taxpayer's account
with the state. A taxpayer generally needs this identifier to file returns, make
payments, obtain a Tax Clearance Certificate, and access other AKIRS services.
Both individuals and businesses operating in the state are expected to be
registered.

## Who needs a Taxpayer ID

Every taxable individual resident in Akwa Ibom State and every business or
company operating in the state should have a Taxpayer ID. Employees have their
tax handled through their employer's PAYE scheme, but they still need a
Taxpayer ID for returns, certificates and compliance. Self-employed persons,
informal-sector operators, and companies all require a Taxpayer ID. From
1 January 2026, all Nigerians are required to possess a Tax Identification
Number (Tax ID) in addition to their National Identification Number (NIN).

## How to register (step by step)

Registration is done online through the AKIRS IbomTax portal (ibomtax.net):

1. **Select taxpayer type** — sign up as an Individual or a Company, choosing the
   account type that suits your needs.
2. **Verify your identity** — the platform performs a quick identity check.
   Individuals only need their NIN to verify identity; BVN is not required.
3. **Get your Taxpayer ID** — as soon as registration is completed, a unique
   Taxpayer ID is generated instantly and is ready to use.

Companies onboarding employees use the corporate flow: sign in or register,
upload the employee list, download the CSV filing templates, then fill and upload
the templates to submit returns.

## Documents and information required

For individual registration, a valid National Identification Number (NIN) is
required for identity verification. Registration typically also involves basic
personal and contact details. Corporate registration involves company
registration details and the employee list for PAYE onboarding. AKIRS collects
personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023; details
are set out in the IbomTax privacy policy.

## The 2026 Tax ID mandate

Under the Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025, effective 1 January 2026 every
Nigerian with a valid NIN will have a Tax ID automatically generated. The Tax ID
is required when seeking financial services. AKIRS has clarified that the
provision does not mean bank accounts of citizens without a Tax ID will be
blocked on 1 January 2026 — it simply requires every taxable Nigerian to provide
a Tax ID when seeking financial services. The Tax ID is intended to ease access
to financial services and improve tax compliance, not to monitor financial
transactions.

## Validating or recovering your Taxpayer ID

If you forget your Taxpayer ID, you can recover it through the IbomTax portal's
sign-in/recovery flow or by contacting AKIRS support at
contactus@akirs.ak.gov.ng. If you cannot remember the generated password, it can
be changed through the platform. A new Taxpayer ID can be generated where a
previous one cannot be recovered. Taxpayers who already registered under the
older TIN/AISTIN arrangements can continue to use their existing Taxpayer ID.
