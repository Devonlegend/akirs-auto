# AKIRS Overview — Akwa Ibom State Internal Revenue Service

## What AKIRS is

The Akwa Ibom State Internal Revenue Service (AKIRS) is the agency responsible
for assessing and collecting taxes and internally generated revenue on behalf of
the Akwa Ibom State Government. Its operational arm is the Akwa Ibom State Board
of Internal Revenue. AKIRS serves taxpayers, businesses, and residents across the
state and administers state taxes under the Akwa Ibom State Revenue
Administration Law, 2016, the Personal Income Tax Act (PITA), and other
applicable Nigerian tax legislation.

## History and legal establishment

The Akwa Ibom State Board of Internal Revenue was established in 2016 when the
then Governor, Mr. Udom Emmanuel, sent a Bill to the Akwa Ibom State House of
Assembly for "a law to make provisions for the administration and collection of
revenue due to the Government of the Akwa Ibom State and to establish the
relevant administrative structures and other matters connected therewith".

The Akwa Ibom State House of Assembly passed the **Akwa Ibom State Revenue
Administration Law, 2016** on 30 June 2016 after all due legislative procedures.
That law established the Akwa Ibom State Board of Internal Revenue (the Board),
whose operational arm is the Akwa Ibom State Internal Revenue Service (AKIRS).
Pioneer Board Executives were appointed and sworn into office on 13 April 2017.

Before the 2016 law, the Service had existed since 1987 as one of the State
Government's extra-ministerial agencies. The 2016 law gave it a clearer legal
mandate, autonomy, and a board structure.

## Vision, mission and core values

**Vision:** To be the leading state internal revenue service provider in Nigeria.

**Mission:** To effectively mobilise Internally Generated Revenue (IGR) by
providing Akwa Ibom State taxpayers with excellent services in a professional,
transparent and accountable manner.

**Core values:** Professionalism, Integrity, Efficiency and Enterprise Spirit.

## Statutory responsibilities

The statutory responsibilities of the Internal Revenue Service, as provided for
by section 85B of the Personal Income Tax Act (1993 as amended) and the Akwa
Ibom State Revenue Administration Law 2016, include:

- Doing all such things as may be deemed necessary and expedient for the
  assessment and collection of tax.
- Accounting for all amounts collected from tax in a manner prescribed by the
  Government.
- Appointing, promoting, transferring and imposing discipline on employees of
  the State Service in accordance with the provisions of the State Civil Service.
- Supervising revenue collection by other Ministries and Departments in the
  State.
- Monitoring Internally Generated Revenue collection in the State.
- Issuing the Akwa Ibom State Taxpayer Identification Number (AISTIN/Taxpayer
  ID), receiving returns, issuing and validating Tax Clearance Certificates
  (TCC), and administering taxpayer registration and compliance.

## Directorates

AKIRS is organised into directorates and units, including:

- **Directorate of Collection** — receives and collates all monies collected as
  tax in the state.
- **Directorate of Assessment** — determines the monies payable as taxes by
  individuals and businesses.
- **Directorate of Other State Taxes** — handles state taxes outside personal
  income tax.
- **PAYE (Directorate of Pay As You Earn)** — collects individual taxes from
  businesses on behalf of their employees.
- **Directorate of Administration/Supplies** — personnel management and the daily
  running of corporate infrastructure across the state.
- **Enforcement/Recovery** — collections from tax defaulters and execution of tax
  court judgments across the state.
- **Finance & Accounts** — fiscal planning and economic affairs of the
  organisation.
- **Directorate of Planning, Research & Strategy** — evidence-based guidance for
  efficient strategies and work implementation.
- **Directorate of Informal Sector** — collection of taxes from registered and
  unregistered businesses in the informal sector.

## Offices and contact

AKIRS's corporate headquarters is Revenue House, Central Business District, Udo
Udoma Avenue, Uyo, Akwa Ibom State. There is also a State Secretariat office at
Block 9, Idongesit Nkanga Secretariat, Abak Road, Uyo, and tax offices across
all Local Government Areas. Opening hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to
4:00 pm. See the "AKIRS Contact and Offices" topic for the full list.

## What the AKIRS Assistant can help with

The AKIRS Assistant can explain the taxes and levies AKIRS administers, how to
register for a Taxpayer ID (AISTIN), how to file returns and make payments
through the IbomTax portal and Akwa Remit, how to obtain and verify a Tax
Clearance Certificate, how to compute personal income tax under the current
rules, and where to find AKIRS offices and support channels. It provides general
guidance only and is not a substitute for an official AKIRS assessment or
professional tax advice.
