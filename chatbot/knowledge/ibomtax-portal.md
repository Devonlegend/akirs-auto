# IbomTax Portal — AKIRS Online Tax Management

## Overview

IbomTax (ibomtax.net) is the official online Tax Management System (TMS) of the
Akwa Ibom State Internal Revenue Service (AKIRS). It is the digital platform
where taxpayers register, file returns, make payments, and obtain and verify Tax
Clearance Certificates (TCC). The platform is described as making taxes less
taxing: "file and relax — pay your taxes, file instantly, and get your
certificate with ease."

## Registration

New users select a taxpayer type — individual or company — and complete a quick
identity check. Individuals only need their NIN to verify identity; BVN is not
required. As soon as registration is completed, a unique Taxpayer ID is
generated instantly. There are separate journeys for individuals, corporations,
and tax professionals/consultants.

## Main features

- **Instant Payments** — safe, seamless tax payments through certified
  electronic channels, with a payment receipt issued immediately.
- **Returns Filing** — file tax returns online without office visits or
  paperwork.
- **Assessments / E-Alerts** — automated assessments and e-alerts with
  notifications about tax obligations and deadlines.
- **Tax Professionals** — connect with certified tax professionals for expert
  advice and support on complex tax scenarios.
- **Tax Calculator** — estimate personal income tax based on the latest Nigerian
  tax regulations and progressive bands, with a step-by-step breakdown. The
  calculator processes data locally and does not store it on the server.
- **Guest Payment** — make a tax payment without an account through a secure
  guest checkout, for one-time payments.
- **Verify TCC** — instantly verify a Tax Clearance Certificate (TCC).

## Corporate filing flow

For corporate taxpayers, IbomTax sets out a four-step filing guide:

1. Sign in or register on ibomtax.net.
2. Upload the employee list to onboard employees to the platform.
3. Download the CSV filing templates for tax returns.
4. Fill in and upload the templates to complete and submit the tax filings.

The portal also publishes important formatting rules that CSV files must follow
to be accepted without errors. Download the current templates and formatting
rules from the portal.

## Payments and Akwa Remit

IbomTax does not collect, store or process payment card details or bank account
credentials. All tax payments are processed exclusively through **Akwa Remit**
(akwaremit.akwaibomstate.gov.ng), the official Akwa Ibom State government
payment platform. IbomTax generates payment references and redirects taxpayers
securely to Akwa Remit. A February 2026 AKIRS notice confirmed that all state
taxes, levies and revenue must be paid through Akwa Remit.

## Data protection and privacy

AKIRS operates IbomTax in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023
and the NDPA General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025. The
platform collects taxpayer data (name, date of birth, contact details,
NIN/BVN, Tax ID, address, occupation, company details) solely for registration,
identity verification, tax assessment, payment facilitation and legal
compliance. Data is not sold or shared for commercial marketing. Tax records are
retained for at least six years from the relevant assessment year. Taxpayers
have rights of access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection,
restriction, withdrawal of consent, and complaint to the Nigerian Data
Protection Commission (NDPC). The Data Protection Officer can be reached at
contactus@akirs.ak.gov.ng.
