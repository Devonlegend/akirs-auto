# Stamp Duties — Akwa Ibom State

## Overview

Stamp duty is a tax paid to confer government and legal recognition on certain
documents and instruments. In Nigeria, stamp duties are governed by the Stamp
Duties Act (SDA) Cap. S8 LFN 2004. Instruments between individuals, partnerships
and enterprises are stamped at the state level (handled by the State Internal
Revenue Service), while instruments executed between corporate bodies are
stamped at the federal level.

## Why documents must be stamped

Documents are stamped to confer government and legal recognition on them, so
that the instrument is duly and properly recognised. Evidence of tax payment is a
prerequisite before stamping, because payment of tax is a civic responsibility
of all adults.

## Who pays stamp duty

The beneficiaries of the transaction pay stamp duty.

## Why stamp duty documents must be verified

Stamp duty documents must be verified to ensure they are duly and properly
stamped by the Stamp Duties Offices.

## Flat rate versus ad valorem

- Stamp duty is **ad valorem** when it is paid on the basis of the value of the
  instrument.
- Stamp duty is **flat** when it is based on fixed charges.

## Types of stampable instruments

Stampable instruments include:

- Deed of Assignment
- Deed of Mortgage
- Power of Attorney
- Deed of Assent
- Vesting Deeds
- Memorandum of Understanding
- Memorandum of Loss
- Contract Agreements
- Tenancy Agreements
- Lease Agreements

## Exemptions

Governments at all levels — Federal, State and Local Governments — and
international agencies are exempt from paying stamp duties. Instruments executed
by government agencies are exempt from stamp duty.

## Which instruments are stamped at state versus federal level

All instruments between individuals, partnerships and enterprises are stamped at
the state level, while instruments executed between corporate bodies are stamped
at the federal level.

## How to pay

Stamp duty due at the state level is payable through AKIRS's authorised
channels. Since February 2026, all state taxes, levies and revenue must be paid
through **Akwa Remit** (akwaremit.akwaibomstate.gov.ng). Taxpayers can also use
the IbomTax portal (ibomtax.net) or visit an AKIRS office.
